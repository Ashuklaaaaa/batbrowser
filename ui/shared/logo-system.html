Logo Usage Rules
  • ✓ DO use the logo at minimum 24px height to maintain legibility
  • ✓ DO maintain the logo's aspect ratio at all times
  • ✓ DO use the dark variant (#00D4B4) as the primary version
  • ✓ DO provide sufficient clear space equal to the bat body height on all sides
  • ✗ DO NOT recolor the bat shape to any color other than the defined brand palette
  • ✗ DO NOT add gradients, textures, or effects directly to the bat path
  • ✗ DO NOT rotate, skew, or distort the logo
  • ✗ DO NOT place the teal logo on colored backgrounds (only dark/light/neutral)
  • ✗ DO NOT use drop shadows directly on the bat shape
  • ✗ DO NOT crop, overlap, or obscure any part of the bat silhouette
